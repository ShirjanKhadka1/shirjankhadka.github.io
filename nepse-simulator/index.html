    How sizing and costs work
    Shares = floor((risk % × equity) / stop distance), whole shares only, never more than cash allows.

    Costs: broker commission (0.36% down to 0.243% by tier), SEBON 0.015%, DP Rs 25 per sell, Rs 5 name transfer per buy. Buy-side costs enter your cost basis; CGT applies only to positive gains. The equity curve is net of everything.

    Limitations

    • Daily closes only; the day's high/low decides whether the stop or target was touched.
    • No slippage — fills are assumed exactly at the close, stop, or target price.
    • Dividends, bonus and rights are not adjusted; ex-date price drops will look like losses.
    • Only securities with full OHLC history can be tested. A profitable backtest does not predict future results.
