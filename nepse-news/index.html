How NEPSE news works here

NEPSE news today, in two streams. The announcements stream carries official company disclosures filed with the exchange — dividend and bonus proposals, rights issues, AGMs and SGMs, book closures, lock-in expiries, mergers and promoter share sales — grouped by recency so today’s filings sit on top. The headlines stream gathers market reporting from Nepal’s business press, always attributed and linked to the publisher; we don’t rewrite their reporting.

Alongside the feeds, the Nepse Decode newsroom publishes verified originals: each story is checked against the official NEPSE or company notice before it goes live, and the underlying document is linked in the article. When we get something wrong, we correct it publicly on the corrections page.

The feeds refresh daily from NEPSE notices, company filings and publisher sites. Headlines are editorially reviewed before they appear, and the update timestamp on the page tells you how fresh the list is. Coverage focuses on listed companies and market-moving policy; it is not exhaustive.

News is information, not advice. An announcement moving a price today says nothing about tomorrow — read the official notice, understand the corporate action, and do your own research before any investment decision.

Every major announcement also gets a permanent story page with the official notice attached, so a dividend declared today is still traceable months later. The corporate-action archive keeps dividends, bonuses, rights and splits searchable by company — useful when you need to know what a board actually approved, not what the rumor mill claimed.

News questions

Where do the company announcements come from?

From the companies’ own filings with NEPSE and official company notices — not rumors or social media posts. Each announcement links to its source document where available.

Do you rewrite other publishers’ news?

No. Headlines are shown as published, attributed and linked to the original publisher. Our own reporting is clearly labeled as Nepse Decode originals.

How often is the news updated?

The feeds refresh daily, and breaking corporate actions publish as they are verified. The timestamp on the page shows the latest update.

Is this investment advice?

No. News and announcements are information for education. Nothing here recommends buying or selling any security.
