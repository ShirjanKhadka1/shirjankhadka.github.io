News questions

Where do the company announcements come from?

From the companies' own filings with NEPSE and official company notices — not rumors or social media posts. Each announcement links to its source document where available.

Do you rewrite other publishers' news?

No. Headlines are shown as published, attributed and linked to the original publisher. Our own reporting is clearly labeled as Nepse Decode originals.

How often is the news updated?

The feeds refresh throughout the day — announcements and headlines publish as they are verified. The timestamp on the page shows the latest update. News has no market-data delay.

Is this investment advice?

No. News and announcements are information for education. Nothing here recommends buying or selling any security.

How NEPSE news works here

NEPSE news today, in two streams. The announcements stream carries official company disclosures filed with the exchange — dividend and bonus proposals, rights issues, AGMs and SGMs, book closures, lock-in expiries, mergers and promoter share sales — newest first. The headlines stream gathers market reporting from Nepal's business press, always attributed and linked to the publisher; we don't rewrite their reporting.

The feeds refresh throughout the day from NEPSE notices, company filings and publisher sites — news has no market-data delay. The update timestamp on the page tells you how fresh the list is. News is information, not advice: an announcement moving a price today says nothing about tomorrow — read the official notice and do your own research before any investment decision.
