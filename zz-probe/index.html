How this page is built. Every figure on this page is a mechanical aggregation of the latest daily batch: tile color and average change come from each security's day change, breadth counts securities that closed higher or lower, leaders and laggards rank by day change, and turnover share divides each sector's session turnover (volume × price) by total turnover. Securities with no day change are left out of breadth, leaders and laggards, and average change. "Unclassified" = securities whose sector is not yet tagged in the data. This is for education only, not investment advice.
