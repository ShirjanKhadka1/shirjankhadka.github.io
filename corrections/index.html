Corrections policy

When we get something wrong, we fix it in the open. This page explains how corrections work and keeps the public record.

How corrections work

When an error is found in one of our stories, we correct it promptly and transparently. The original mistake is never silently erased: the corrected story notes what changed and when, and the correction is entered in the public log below.

Correction vs clarification

A correction fixes a factual error, such as a wrong number, a wrong date, or the wrong company. A clarification adds context without changing any fact. Both appear in the log.
