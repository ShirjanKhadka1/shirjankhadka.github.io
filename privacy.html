Privacy Policy

Effective: September 28, 2026

This site, shirjankhadka.com.np, is a personal portfolio and a set of free finance tools. This policy explains what data is collected here. The short version: almost nothing.

We do not run accounts, sign-ups, newsletters, or contact forms. The only personal information you share with us is what you send voluntarily by email (for example, tool feedback sent to the address on the contact page). We use it only to reply, and we never sell or share it.

Cookies

This site itself does not set tracking cookies. If advertising is introduced in the future (for example, Google AdSense), the ad provider may use cookies to serve and measure ads. You can control cookies through your browser settings at any time.

Advertising

When ads are shown on this site, they are served by third-party vendors such as Google. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.

External links

This site links to external websites (market data sources, LinkedIn, and others). Their privacy practices are their own; this policy covers only shirjankhadka.com.np.

Children's privacy

This site is a general-audience educational resource and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
