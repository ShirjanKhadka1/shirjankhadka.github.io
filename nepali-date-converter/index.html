About the Nepali Date Converter

Nepali Date Converter (Miti converter) converts dates between Bikram Sambat (BS), the official calendar of Nepal, and the Gregorian calendar (AD), instantly and in both directions. It is useful for converting a date of birth, historical Nepali dates, passport and visa dates, and dates in document translation.

How BS to AD conversion works

The Bikram Sambat calendar does not follow a fixed formula: month lengths vary from year to year. This converter uses a published BS month-length dataset for BS 2000–2090 (14 April 1943 AD – 13 April 2034 AD), the same reference data used by popular open-source Nepali date libraries, verified against known anchors such as 2000-01-01 BS = 14 April 1943 AD.

English to Nepali Date Converter (AD to BS)

To convert an English date to a Nepali date, switch the source calendar to AD, pick the year, month and day, and the Bikram Sambat equivalent appears instantly. This direction is what most people need for citizenship dates of birth, passport applications, and filling Nepali forms with English-calendar dates.

Nepali to English Date Converter (BS to AD)

To convert a Nepali date to an English date, keep BS selected as the source and choose the Nepali year, month and day, the Gregorian (AD) equivalent shows immediately. Useful for visa forms, WES/ECE credential evaluations, and translating dates on Nepali documents.

More than a BS to AD converter

Use the Difference tab to find the exact gap between any two Nepali or English dates in years, months and days, and the Age tab as a Nepali age calculator, enter a date of birth in BS or AD to get the precise age, total days lived, and the next birthday.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert a BS date to AD?

Keep BS selected as the source calendar, pick the year, month and day from the dropdowns, the equivalent Gregorian (AD) date appears instantly below. No convert button needed.

How do I convert an AD (English) date to Nepali date?

Switch the source calendar to AD, choose the English date, and the Bikram Sambat equivalent appears instantly in the result card.

How accurate is this converter?

It uses a published Bikram Sambat month-length dataset for BS 2000–2090, the same reference data behind popular open-source Nepali date libraries, verified against known anchors (e.g. 2000-01-01 BS = 14 April 1943 AD) and 2,000 round-trip conversion tests.

What is aaja ko miti (today's Nepali date)?

The converter opens on today's date in both calendars, so the current Nepali date (aaja ko miti) is always shown at the top of the page without any input.

What date range is supported?

Bikram Sambat 2000 to 2090, corresponding to 14 April 1943 AD – 13 April 2034 AD.

Can I calculate age from a Nepali date of birth?

Yes, the Age tab is a Nepali age calculator. Enter the date of birth in BS or AD to get the exact age in years, months and days, plus total days lived and a countdown to the next birthday.

How does the age calculator BS work?

The Age tab works as a dedicated age calculator for BS dates: pick the date of birth in Bikram Sambat (or AD), and it computes the exact age in years, months and days against today's BS date, with total days lived and the next birthday countdown. All calculations use the same verified BS 2000–2090 calendar data.
