How the warning radar is built

Every rule, every factor, every validation number — including what the engine gets wrong. If a methodology page won't show you its failures, it isn't one.

A warning instrument, not a trading system

Verdict Engine v2 scans every NEPSE stock's price history for operator footprints — the recurring shapes left by pump-and-distribution campaigns — and attaches the evidence to every flag. It answers "which names look dangerous, and why?"

What it does not do: issue buy/sell calls, promise returns, or trade for you. We tested converting its warnings into mechanical exit rules across 251 stocks with full NEPSE costs — the median edge over buy-and-hold was exactly zero. So we don't sell it as a trading system. Its proven value is the warning itself: 89.4% of historical pump episodes were flagged 5 sessions before the peak.

The confidence cap: no verdict ever exceeds 0.90 confidence. Nothing on this page claims certainty — certainty about markets is always a sales pitch, never a measurement.

The nine engine rules

Derived from NEPSE's own data — 365 symbols, up to 750 sessions each, June 2023 to October 2026 — not imported from foreign markets.

1

Age-gated circuits

Repeated limit-ups mean ignition for listings under ~15 sessions old, and exhaustion above it. The sign flips at 13–15 sessions — measured, not assumed.

2

Regime gates

Circuit, markup and volume-surge factors score only in bull regimes — they invert sideways. A flat market mutes the warnings honestly instead of firing them blindly.

3

Slow-bleed stays experimental

Quiet distribution detection works only in bear regimes and stays out of the score until it earns its way in. Shown, never scored.

4

Upweighted reversals

20-day winner-fade and volume surge carry extra weight — they tested ~3× stronger per-symbol than cross-sectionally.

5

Circuits select, don't time

Circuit clustering picks which stocks are pump-shaped across the market; it is a weak timer for any single stock.

6

Sector honesty

Finance and development-bank stocks actively contradict the bearish factors — their readings are shown, not scored. Hydropower gets half weight.

7

Young-listing lenses

Volume factors are blind in a new listing's first weeks, so short-horizon lenses cover the gap instead of pretending the standard ones work.

8

No breakout worship

A heavy-volume supply zone breaking upward is not scored as fuel — the median new-listing breakout fails. Breakouts need confirmation.

9

Tick-aware circuits

Limit-ups are detected at 0.995× the band for ignition runs — NEPSE prints settle a tick under the theoretical limit.

The six factors, with their measured stats

C

Circuit clustering

Limit-up days in 60 sessions. Cross-sectional IC −0.076 — the strongest pump-top flag: it fired before 16 of 16 labeled episodes (5.4× lift over base rate).

M

Markup asymmetry

60-day run-up ÷ max drawdown. IC −0.051; caught 14/16 episodes. Parabolic, pullback-free advances are the operator footprint — healthy trends breathe.

W

Winner fade (inverted momentum)

20-day cross-sectional return, faded. Per-symbol IC −0.102; 80% of symbols negative. On NEPSE, hot stocks cool — the opposite of foreign momentum lore.

V

Volume surge

20-day vs 120-day turnover. Per-symbol IC −0.086. On NEPSE, abnormal volume is distribution more often than accumulation.

T

Turnover spike

Single-day vs 60-day median. IC −0.037. Climax volume predicts 5–10 session weakness — the T+2-specific version of this story was tested and rejected.

S

Slow-bleed v2 (experimental)

Range compression × volume, no circuits. IC −0.015 with 42% of symbols contradicting — shown as evidence, never in the score.

Rejected hypotheses are published too: the T+2 settlement-lag effect didn't survive the data, and listing age alone predicts nothing. A factor that fails is a finding, not an embarrassment.

Validation — the numbers we stand behind

89%

Episode flag rate

59 of 66 pump episodes flagged 5 sessions before the peak (15/16 on the labeled set; the 16th was correctly refused as poisoned data, not missed).

−1.8

Walk-forward spread

Flagged days fell −3.33% over the next 20 sessions vs −1.58% unflagged (70.6% of flagged days negative). Direction correct; the flag is rare by design (~2% of days).

0

Mechanical edge

Turning warnings into exit rules: median ΔCAGR 0.00pp vs buy-and-hold, full costs. This is why the engine is a warning instrument — and why we say so on every page.

What we refuse to do: score poisoned data (impossible single-day moves from unadjusted corporate actions return UNRELIABLE, never a guess); publish lock-in expiries from proxy dates (the HATHY precedent — a 12-day error that flipped the entire read); show a signal without its failure rate.

Caveats — read before citing a number

Survivorship: listed symbols only. 750-session window (2023-06 → 2026-10). Prices unadjusted for corporate actions. Daily bars only — no intraday, no order book. 45 symbols lack history and are excluded, never invented. The age-crossover rests on 57 listings — significant, but young. ICs measure signal strength, not profit.
