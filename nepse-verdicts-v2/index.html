Reading guide

How to read a verdict

Tap any row to see the full proof. Here is what each label means, in plain words:

!

Distribution warning

Several danger signs are flashing at once — the price action looks like big players selling their shares to excited late buyers. In the past, 7 out of 10 stocks flagged like this fell within the next 20 trading days.

△

Elevated risk

An early warning — one or two danger signs are starting to show. Worth watching closely, not a full pattern yet.

○

Neutral

No danger signs right now. The engine sees nothing alarming — that calm is itself useful information.

?

Unreliable

The price data itself looks broken (impossible jumps from unadjusted bonus/right-share changes). The engine refuses to guess rather than mislead you.

The honest truth: in backtests this engine flagged about 9 out of 10 historical pump-and-dump episodes 5 days before the peak — but it still misses some, and a flag is never a buy or sell order. Its proven value is simple: it tells you which stocks look dangerous and why, so you can be careful. Past patterns do not guarantee future results.
