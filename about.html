About Shirjan Khadka

Kathmandu, Nepal

I am a finance graduate (BBM, Tribhuvan University, 3.82 CGPA) from Kathmandu, working toward a career as a financial analyst. This website is my public workspace: the tools I build, the research I do, and what I learn about markets, shared openly.

Why this site exists

Good market tools in Nepal mostly sit behind paywalls that cost thousands of rupees. I am building professional-grade NEPSE tools and giving them away for free: a daily stock screener covering every listed security, the Alpha Lab chart workspace, a data-quality dashboard, and daily finance research. If a student or a small investor finds them useful, the site has done its job.

How the tools work

Every signal on this site comes from transparent, rule-based logic, trend, momentum, volume, chart structure, computed from market data and shown with its reasoning. There is no black box. The screener even publishes each buy call's historical hit rate, so you can see exactly where the engine has worked and where it has not.

What this site is not

Nothing here is investment advice, a stock tip, or a recommendation to buy or sell. The tools are educational. Markets carry risk, and you should do your own research, or talk to a licensed advisor, before making any investment decision.
