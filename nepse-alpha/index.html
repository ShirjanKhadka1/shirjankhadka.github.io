Data

Real data, honestly labeled.

NEPSE publishes no free official real-time API, so tick-level data like broker terminals show is not possible here. What you get instead is stated plainly, with sources you can check.

Daily OHLC · open Nepse-All-Scraper dataset Live quotes · yonepse open API Index history 2003–2026 · nepse-open-data archive News · attributed publisher headlines

Delayed, not fake

Quotes come from free community mirrors during market hours and can lag the official feed. The page says so, and the ticker stays hidden rather than showing stale numbers as live.

Every listing, flagged honestly

All 410 listed securities are covered. Newer listings without full OHLC history use LTP-only data and are clearly flagged, with pattern detection disabled for those.

Computed in your browser

Indicators, patterns and verdicts are calculated client-side from the data files. No account, no tracking, nothing uploaded.

Questions

Before you ask.

Does this give buy or sell recommendations?

It shows a mechanical verdict, Strong Buy to Strong Exit, with every contributing factor listed. It is a starting point for your own research, not investment advice, and not an AI prediction.

Is the data real-time?

Quotes refresh during NEPSE trading hours (Monday–Friday, 11:00–15:00 NPT) from free community mirrors and can be delayed. Outside hours you see the last closed session. Daily history is OHLC per stock.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything runs in your browser, free, with no login and no tracking.

Which patterns are detected?

Double tops and bottoms, head & shoulders (and inverse), ascending, descending and symmetrical triangles, rising and falling wedges, plus regular and hidden bullish/bearish divergences on RSI(14) and the MACD histogram.

Where does the data come from?

Per-stock daily OHLC from the open Nepse-All-Scraper dataset, live quotes and monthly LTP history from the yonepse open API, and NEPSE index daily history (2003–2026) from the nepse-open-data archive. Coverage is verified on the data coverage page.
