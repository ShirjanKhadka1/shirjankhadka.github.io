    About the NEPSE watchlist

    The NEPSE watchlist is your personal shortlist of the market: add any listed security and see its latest price, day change, the engine’s signal and its RSI(14) in one row. Sort and filter the list, remove names you no longer follow, and jump straight to the full candlestick chart for any symbol with one tap.

    Beside it sits the engine watchlist, curated automatically — securities the rule-based engine currently rates Strong Buy or Buy, refreshed with each daily batch. Treat it as a starting point for research, not a recommendation.

    Your list is saved in your browser, unlimited and free, with no login and nothing uploaded anywhere. Figures come from the latest daily batch: NEPSE publishes no free official real-time API, so prices can lag during trading hours and are final only after the close.

    Signals and RSI readings are mechanical measurements for education. A Strong Buy verdict is the engine’s summary of ten scored chart factors, not a tip — do your own research before any investment decision.

    A watchlist is also a discipline tool: by writing down the names you follow and the levels that matter, you trade your plan instead of the day’s noise. Review the engine’s verdict column against your own thesis, let the RSI column flag when a favorite drifts into overbought or oversold territory — then open the chart and decide for yourself.

    Watchlist questions

    How many stocks can I add to the watchlist?

    Unlimited. The list lives in your browser’s local storage, so there is no cap and no account — but clearing browser data erases it.

    What do the signal and RSI columns mean?

    Signal is the rule-based engine’s verdict (Strong Buy to Strong Exit) from ten scored chart factors; RSI(14) below 30 reads oversold and above 70 overbought by convention. Both are mechanical, educational measurements.

    Is the watchlist data real-time?

    No. Rows refresh from the latest daily batch and can lag during market hours. NEPSE publishes no free official real-time API; community mirrors may be delayed.

    Does the watchlist sync across devices?

    No. It is stored only in the browser where you created it. There is no login and no server copy.
