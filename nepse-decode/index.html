One free suite for the whole NEPSE workflow

Nepse Decode is a free suite of NEPSE tools built for how Nepali investors actually work: check the market on the dashboard, scan for ideas in the screener, study the chart, and track what you own — all without a login or paywall. The dashboard brings the NEPSE index, top gainers and losers, most-active stocks, trending names, the latest engine verdicts and the newsroom’s headlines into one page.

Behind it sits one transparent, rule-based signal engine: ten scored factors — trend versus moving averages, RSI, MACD, turnover, 52-week position, divergences, chart patterns, market backdrop and support/resistance — add up to a verdict from Strong Buy to Strong Exit, with every factor’s contribution shown. The same engine powers the chart workspace, the daily full-market screener and the sector views, so a signal means the same thing everywhere.

Data comes from open archives and free community mirrors of NEPSE market data, rebuilt into a daily batch after each trading session. NEPSE publishes no free official real-time API, so quotes can be delayed during market hours and are final only after the close; every page carries its session date so a stale figure is never presented as current.

The site is built and maintained by Shirjan Khadka, a finance graduate in Kathmandu, as a public workspace — professional-grade NEPSE tools given away free, because good market tools in Nepal mostly sit behind paywalls. Everything is educational and never investment advice: verify with your broker before trading.

Nepse Decode questions

What NEPSE tools are included?

Candlestick charts with a pattern and divergence scanner, a daily full-market screener with ranked signals, sector performance, a portfolio tracker, a watchlist, verified market news, broker analytics and daily finance research — all free, no login.

Is the market data real-time?

No. Data is rebuilt into a daily batch after each trading session; during market hours quotes come from free community mirrors and can be delayed. NEPSE publishes no free official real-time API. Every page shows its session date.

How does the signal engine work?

Ten transparent factors are scored on the daily chart and summed into a verdict from Strong Buy to Strong Exit, with every factor’s contribution listed. It is a mechanical illustration for education, not investment advice.

Who builds Nepse Decode?

Shirjan Khadka, a finance graduate (BBM, Tribhuvan University) in Kathmandu. The site is his public workspace: the tools he builds and the research he does, shared openly and free.
