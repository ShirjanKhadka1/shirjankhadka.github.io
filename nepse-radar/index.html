How does the Warning Radar work?

The v2 verdict engine analyses price action, volume patterns, and market regime for every NEPSE-listed equity daily. Distribution Warning flags stocks showing operator distribution footprints (heavy selling into strength). Elevated Risk flags deteriorating technical structure. Bounce Setup flags oversold conditions with reversal potential. All rules are transparent and backtested. This is educational, not investment advice.
