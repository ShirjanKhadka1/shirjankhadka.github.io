Stock–Sector Sentiment

How has each stock been behaving against its own sector? This board answers with a single 0–100 sentiment score built from three published components — relative momentum (the stock's return minus its sector's), up-day edge (how much more often it closes up than its sector does) and flow-pressure edge (whether its volume has been arriving on up days, again versus its sector). The construction is Nepse Decode's own, written out in full below — and the measured replay further down shows what high and low readings have actually been followed by since 2021, before you read anything into one.

      Methodology

      How this board is built

      A descriptive reading of recent price and volume behaviour against each stock's sector, for education, not investment advice. Data: Nepse Decode's own OHLC archive, rebuilt after each close. No live feed is claimed: during market hours this page still shows the last close's board.
