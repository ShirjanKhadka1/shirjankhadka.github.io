Our commitment

Editorial standards

The rules every Nepse Decode story follows. They are public so readers can hold us to them.

What we publish

Verified financial news about NEPSE-listed companies and the Nepali capital market: dividends, bonus shares, right shares, listings, promoter activity, and regulatory decisions that affect investors. Everything is free, with no login required.

Verification

Every market-moving claim must trace to an official document: NEPSE, SEBON, Nepal Rastra Bank, or the company's own disclosure. Secondary reporting is labeled as such and is never presented as verified fact. A story does not publish until its core claim clears this check.

What we never publish

  • Rumors or unconfirmed market talk
  • Price predictions or targets
  • Buy or sell recommendations
  • Paid content presented as news

Photography

Real licensed photographs only, credited to their photographer and source. We never use AI-generated news images.

Bylines

The organization byline "Nepse Decode Newsroom" means the story passed human review by the owner before publication. We publish no invented reporters and no borrowed names.

Updates

Stories are updated as facts develop, with a visible correction and update history on each story page.

Reporting an error

If you spot a mistake, please tell us so we can fix it. See our corrections policy for how to report an error and where every correction is logged.
