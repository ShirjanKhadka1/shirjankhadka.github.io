How to read this table. Signals come from a rule-based engine (trend, RSI, MACD, turnover, 52-week position, divergences, chart patterns, market backdrop, support/resistance) — the same engine behind the Alpha Lab. Stop-loss = last close − 2 × ATR(14); target = last close + 4 × ATR(14), a 1:2 risk-reward frame. “Setup” names the most recent chart pattern or divergence detected.

Rule-based screening for education only — not investment advice. Signals computed from the last close (–). Securities with under 60 sessions of history get no signal.
