          How to read this table. Signals come from a rule-based engine (trend, RSI, MACD, turnover, 52-week position, divergences, chart patterns, market backdrop, support/resistance), the same Alpha Lab engine behind Nepse Decode. Buy signals are gated: no Buy is issued while the price sits below its 200-day average, entries only fire in long-term stock uptrends. Hit rates are regime-dependent, and both regimes are published: across the older 250-session window (NEPSE +31.0%) the engine’s buy calls hit 31.0%; across the most recent 250 sessions (NEPSE −6.2%) they hit 11.4%. Stop-loss = last close − 2 × ATR(14); target = last close + 4 × ATR(14), a 1:2 risk-reward frame. “Setup” names the most recent chart pattern or divergence detected. Hit rate replays the engine’s past Buy/Strong Buy calls on equity securities over the last 250 sessions: a call is a hit when its own target was touched before its stop within the next 20 sessions. Calls touching neither level are excluded; under 5 decided calls shows “–”.

          Rule-based screening for education only, not investment advice. Signals computed from the last close (–). Securities with under 60 sessions of history get no signal.
