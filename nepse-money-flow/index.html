Does following the flow beat the market?

Forward tracking, honest numbers.

Loading tracking…

Method: each session's accumulated stocks (top-20 pairs, deduplicated) and distributed stocks are walked forward 5 / 10 / 20 of their own trading sessions. Excess return is measured against the equal-weight average stock that session; any window containing a single-session move over 15% is dropped as corporate-action noise. The headline is the mean of session means — never pooled across sessions of different sizes. Nothing here is a buy or sell call.

How to read this page

Source

Public NEPSE floorsheet records, pulled server-side after the post-close finality check (stable totals, no preliminary snapshot). Served from Nepse Decode's own archive — never proxied.

What net flow is

Buy value minus sell value for a broker, or for one stock through one broker. It is transaction flow in the session — not a holding, not a position, and not evidence of intent.

What it is not

Broker rankings are not skill rankings. A top net buyer one session is usually not the top buyer the next — across 62 tracked sessions only about 3 of the top 10 repeat.

Archive

One snapshot per session, 2026-07-15 onward, append-only. A session's snapshot is never rewritten when later sessions arrive — the tracking proof is that old cells stay byte-identical.

Tracking honesty

Groups are lenses, not partitions: a stock can appear in both lists. Pending horizons are shown as pending, never estimated. The formula never changes to flatter the result.

Limits

Broker flow covers only trades that printed on the floorsheet. Off-market transfers, promoter pledges and holdings are outside this data and outside these claims.
