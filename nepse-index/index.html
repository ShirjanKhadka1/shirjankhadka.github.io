How this page is built

Sources. The level, day range and sub-indices come from the published NEPSE quote snapshot (live.json); the chart and session tables come from the site index archive of daily closes (2003–2026, index-history.json); the intraday path comes from the session's published index observations (intraday-index.json). Turnover is the sum of the session's published quote turnovers, and breadth counts quote day-change signs — the same definitions the daily page uses, so the figures match it exactly.

Delay honesty. The market feed is a delayed free feed. The freshness line shows the data's own timestamp and measured age; a closed session is labeled CLOSED and never repaints as live. During market hours this page refetches every 60 seconds and repaints only when the index value, timestamp or quote count changes.

What is not shown. The index archive publishes no open and no volume for the index itself, so neither appears here. The static block above is the no-JavaScript fallback for the 28 Oct 2026 close; it is hand-maintained and will stale at the next session until the noscript baker covers this page.
