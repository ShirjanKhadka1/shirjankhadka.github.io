Technical Signals

Eleven standard indicators grade every stock on this board — MACD, RSI, the moving-average ladders, Bollinger Bands, Bull/Bear Power, Supertrend, Ichimoku, Keltner, ADX and Parabolic SAR — each one Strong Buy, Buy, Neutral, Sell or Strong Sell, and the votes combine into a single 0–100 score. Nothing here is a black box: open any stock to see every indicator value behind its grades, and read the measured replay further down before reading anything into a Strong Buy — it shows, session by session since 2021, what these grades were actually worth.

How this board is built

Mechanical indicator grades on past prices, for education, not investment advice. Data: Nepse Decode's own OHLC archive, rebuilt after each close. No live feed is claimed: during market hours this page still shows the last close's board.
