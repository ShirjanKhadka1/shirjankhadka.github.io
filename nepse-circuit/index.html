How to read this

  • Circuit limit ±10%: NEPSE's standard price band for most listed securities, measured from the previous close. A few securities may carry different bands — the exchange's circulars are the authority.
  • Distance to circuit = 10% minus the absolute % change. A stock at +9.2% is 0.8 points from the upper circuit.
  • Delayed data: this page reads the same ~15-minute delayed public snapshot as the rest of Nepse Decode. It is not a real-time feed — true real-time data requires NEPSE's licensed API.
  • Auto-refresh: the table re-checks every 30 seconds during market hours (Mon–Fri 11:00–15:00 NPT) and every 5 minutes outside hours.
