How this page is built. Every figure on this page is a mechanical aggregation of the latest daily batch: tile color comes from each security's day change, breadth counts securities that closed higher or lower, leaders and laggards rank by day change, and turnover share divides each sector's session turnover by total turnover. Securities with no day change are left out of breadth, leaders and laggards, and average change. The grouping is honest about its limits: Debentures, Mutual Funds and Promoter Shares are instrument types, not NEPSE sectors (taken from the universe instrument-type field, NEPSE debenture symbol suffixes, and bond/rinpatra/fund/kosh in the official company name). Banking, Hydropower and the other named groups are NEPSE sub-indexes, mapped per symbol from explicit business words in the official company name plus verified overrides for symbols whose names do not reveal the business (for example BNL and UNL in manufacturing, SCBD and SHINED as debentures, Chandragiri Hills and Kalinchowk Darshan in hotels and tourism). Others is NEPSE's own Others sub-index bucket (for example NTC, NRM, Nepal Warehousing). Every listed security sits in exactly one group. This is for education only, not investment advice.
