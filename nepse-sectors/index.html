How this page is built. Every figure on this page is a mechanical aggregation of the latest daily batch: tile color comes from each security's day change, tile size from its session turnover (volume × price), breadth counts securities that closed higher or lower, leaders and laggards rank by day change, and turnover share divides each sector's session turnover by total turnover. Securities with no day change are left out of breadth, leaders and laggards, and average change. The grouping is honest about its limits: Debentures, Mutual Funds and Promoter Shares are instrument types, not NEPSE sectors (taken from the universe instrument-type field, NEPSE debenture symbol suffixes, and bond/rinpatra/fund/kosh in the official company name). Banking, Hydropower and the other named groups are NEPSE sub-indexes inferred from explicit business words in the official company name. Unclassified = securities the data cannot group: opaque symbols with no usable company name (for example BNL, SCBD, SHINED) and businesses outside the sub-index keywords (for example NTC, NRM, NRN). This is for education only, not investment advice.
