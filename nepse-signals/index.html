Why publish a losing system? Because honesty is the product. The 2.2-year backtest shows NEPSE is a trend market — momentum worked, mean-reversion lost money. We publish all three, winners and losers, so you can see exactly what the data says instead of a cherry-picked highlight reel.
How to read these. Every number comes from a mechanical replay of the exact published rules on historical NEPSE data (2024-09-01 to 2026-10-30, 315 symbols, Rs 1 Cr portfolio). Nothing is hand-picked. Live mini-records count decided trades only (n); open trades are shown separately and never enter n, the win rate, or profit factor. Live win rates carry a Wilson 95% interval, and below n=30 the too-few-trades badge applies. Past performance does not predict future results. Read the full methodology →
