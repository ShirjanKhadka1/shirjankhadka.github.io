What this chart shows — and what it doesn’t

Every candle is a real NEPSE trading session: per-stock daily OHLC from the open Nepse-All-Scraper archive, the NEPSE index back to 2003, and the session in progress appended from our own market snapshots while the market is open (Mon–Fri, 11:00–15:00 NPT). Moving averages are computed in your browser from those closes — nothing hidden, nothing invented.

Drawing tools (trend lines, Fibonacci) are not part of this chart: it runs on TradingView’s free open-source Lightweight Charts engine, which ships without the annotation toolkit. For pattern detection and rule-based signals, see the Chart & signals lab.

Data delayed ~15 minutes, like the rest of Nepse Decode. Securities without a full OHLC archive are clearly flagged as LTP-only. Educational use only — not investment advice.

Is this free? Do I need an account?

Yes, free — no account, no login. Your symbol and overlay choices are remembered in your own browser only.

Where does the data come from?

Per-stock daily OHLC from the open Nepse-All-Scraper dataset, NEPSE index history (2003–present) from the nepse-open-data archive, and the live session from our own market snapshots.

Why no drawing tools?

This chart is built on TradingView’s free open-source Lightweight Charts engine, which doesn’t include the annotation toolkit — so what you get is fast, honest price action with moving averages instead.
