Price Push

When a stock rises, whose money pushed it? This board answers per stock: the brokers that net-bought it over the window, ranked by net amount, lead pusher first, each pusher's share of the net buying beside their money — plus the price impact per crore of net buying. Both are descriptions of what happened, not proof of what caused it: every buy has a seller, and flow beside a rise is not proof the flow made the rise.

    How this board is built

    A descriptive reading of broker flow beside price moves, for education, not investment advice. Data: Nepse Decode's own floorsheet archive, rebuilt after each close. No live feed is claimed: during market hours this page shows the last close's board, and refreshes itself when a new board is published.
