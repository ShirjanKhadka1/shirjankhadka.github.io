Price Push

When a stock rises, whose money pushed it? This board answers per stock: the brokers that net-bought it over the window, ranked by net amount, with the lead pusher first and each pusher's share of the net buying printed beside their money. A second number — the price impact per crore of net buying — says how many percentage points the price moved for each crore that went in. Both are descriptions of what happened, not claims about what caused it: every buy has a seller, and flow beside a rise is not proof the flow made the rise.

    How this board is built
