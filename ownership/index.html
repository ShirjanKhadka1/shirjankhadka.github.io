Ownership and funding

Who runs Nepse Decode, how it is paid for, and what that means for our coverage.

Ownership

Nepse Decode is an independent financial information service operated by Shirjan Khadka (Kathmandu, Nepal).

Funding

Nepse Decode is supported by advertising revenue. There is no paywall, no paid subscription, no sponsored article, and no payment is accepted for coverage. Editorial decisions are made independently of advertisers.

Mission

Free, verified financial information for Nepali investors: what happened, what the official record says, and what it means per share, without hype and without a price tag.
