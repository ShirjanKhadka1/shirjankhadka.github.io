NEPSE Calendar

Every date that moves the NEPSE calendar in one place: market holidays, promoter share lock-in unlocks, IPO and FPO opening and closing dates, and book closures. The calendar is drawn live from this site's own data files — the same files behind the IPO tracker and the lock-in dataset — and every event shows its source. A date we cannot source is not shown; it is never guessed.

How this calendar is built

One events database feeds this page, merged in your browser with two supplements — there is no second copy to drift out of date. The calendar events database (Nepse Decode's builder-maintained file, 200+ events from 2025 to 2029) carries market holidays, promoter lock-in expiries, IPO and FPO openings and closings, right-share book closures, dividends and AGMs — every event with its own source and a verified or unverified status, both shown in the day view. The MF & QII lock-in supplement (the reserved IPO quota held by mutual funds and qualified institutional investors) comes from the Nepse Alpha lock-in tracker, retrieved 2026-10-23 — 32 upcoming unlocks that the main database does not carry. Cross-checks against ShareSansar differ by one day on some symbols (a lock-in-end vs first-tradable-day convention); dates are exactly as the named source publishes them. Project-affected local shares in hydropower and manufacturing issues carry the same three-year lock as promoter shares (ShareSansar's stated rule), so for those companies the promoter date is also the local unlock date. The promoter lock-in table above comes from the lock-in dataset (Capital Max IPO Lock-in Period, transcribed 2026-10-05): 100 companies, including those whose end date the source has not published — they are listed as “not published in source”, never guessed. Saturdays and Sundays are shaded as market-closed (NEPSE trades Monday–Friday), and each day cell shows its Bikram Sambat date from this site's own converter table (2083 verified against the official calendar).
