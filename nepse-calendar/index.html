How is this calendar built?

Holidays follow the Government of Nepal gazette lists and NEPSE’s own closure confirmations; entries not yet confirmed by a NEPSE notice are marked Unverified. Issue windows come from SEBON approvals and issue-manager notices. Dividend announcements, book closes and AGMs are recorded from official company disclosures the day they are announced. Promoter lock-in expiries are derived from each IPO’s allotment date plus the three-year SEBON lock-in, and are labelled Derived unless an official notice verifies them. Lock-in expiries for mutual-fund and institutional tranches are not published here — our records do not hold verified dates for them.

About the NEPSE calendar

NEPSE runs on the Bikram Sambat calendar, and the dates that decide an investor’s week — a book close that cuts off dividend eligibility, an issue window that opens and shuts, a promoter lock-in that releases a large block of shares — are scattered across dozens of company notices. This calendar gathers them into one place, dated in BS with the AD date alongside, and states where each entry came from.

Promoter lock-in expiries deserve a careful read. When a lock-in ends, shares that could not be traded for three years become tradable; that is a supply event worth knowing about in advance, not a signal in either direction. Dates marked derived are computed from allotment records and the standard three-year rule — an official release notice, when one is published, is listed separately and takes precedence.

The calendar is an educational reference. Dates announced by companies can change; always confirm a deadline against the official notice before acting on it.
