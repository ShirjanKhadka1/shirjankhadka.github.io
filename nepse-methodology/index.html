Methodology

How every engine on this site works, what it has actually done forward, and what is still unproven. No accuracy figure on this page is hand-written: the forward ledgers below are rendered from the site's own live paper-trade ledger, and anything that has not cleared the promotion gates is labeled as pending — never filled in with a plausible number.

Read this first. Everything on this site is educational, not investment advice. Signals are mechanical readings of past prices; past patterns do not guarantee future results. A verdict is a rules-based summary, not a prediction, and never a guaranteed call.

1 · Engine verdict (chart + screener)

Each listed security gets a daily score from ten factors, all computed from its own price history:

  • Trend vs SMA 50 (±1.5) and vs SMA 200 (±1.5); SMA 20/50 crossover in the last 20 sessions (±2)
  • RSI(14) zones (overbought −1, firm above 55 +0.5, soft below 45 −0.5); MACD histogram direction (±1 to −1)
  • Turnover surge (+1) or drought (−0.5); position within the 52-week range (±0.5 to −1)
  • Divergences (bullish +2; bearish −1.5 regular, −1 hidden); chart patterns (±1) — only confirmed patterns score; a forming pattern is labeled as forming and scores 0
  • Market backdrop: NEPSE index above or below its 200-day average (±0.5); support/resistance proximity (±0.5)

Bands: score ≥ 5 Strong Buy; ≥ 2 Buy; above −2 Hold; above −5 Exit / Reduce; otherwise Strong Exit. Regime gate: a Buy or Strong Buy is blocked to Hold while the price sits below its 200-day average — no longs in a long-term downtrend.

How to read a pattern label next to a Buy. The setup label names the most recent pattern. Only a confirmed pattern subtracts points; a forming pattern scores 0. So a Buy shown next to a bearish pattern name is either a forming pattern (display state, not a logic contradiction) or — where the pattern is confirmed — a case the score's other factors outweighed. The engine does not hide these cases; the screener shows them, and the promotion-gated fix for confirmed-bearish Buys is pending (see §5).

2 · Signal systems (momentum, trend-relay, reversal)

  • Momentum: close above the 50-day SMA with the 20-day SMA above the 50-day SMA; trigger is a 50-session highest close; RSI(14) between 55 and 80; 20-day average turnover above Rs 10 lakh. Stop 2 × ATR(14); trail on a break of the 20-session lowest close.
  • Trend-relay: price below the 50-day SMA for at least 20 of the last 30 sessions, then the 20-day SMA crosses above the 50-day SMA with volume over 1.5 × the 20-day average; 20-day average turnover above Rs 10 lakh. Stop 2 × ATR(14); trail on a cross back or 3 closes under the 50-day SMA.
  • Reversal: RSI(14) below 30 with the close below the lower Bollinger Band (20, 2); trigger is a bullish candle closing in the top half of its range; 20-day average turnover above Rs 10 lakh. Stop 1.5 × ATR(14); exit at the mean (20-day SMA).
  • Targets (T1/T2) are reference levels, not traded exits. Forward paper trades exit on Target 1, stop, ratcheting trailing stop, or a 20-session time exit, at Rs 10 lakh notional with 0.5% round-trip charges and no lookahead.

4 · Warning radar, technical board, simulator

  • Warning radar (verdicts-v2): distribution and elevated-risk warnings per security per session, scored at a 20-session horizon. Warnings with no resolved horizon yet are shown as open, never as hits or misses.
  • Technical board: an 11-indicator vote scored 0–100 (≤41 Strong Sell … ≥70 Strong Buy). Cohort outcomes are tracked 5 and 10 sessions forward and stay pending until those sessions pass.
  • Simulator costs: tiered brokerage (0.36% ≤ Rs 50k down to 0.243% above Rs 1 Cr, minimum Rs 10), SEBON 0.015% both sides, DP Rs 25 per sell, transfer Rs 5 per buy, CGT 5% (≤365 days) / 3.75% (over 365 days) on positive gains only, buy costs in basis. Signal backtests use a simplified flat 0.5% round-trip instead — so backtest profit factors are slightly optimistic against the simulator's own tiered model, and the two are never presented as the same cost basis.

5 · Accuracy status — what is pending, and why no number is shown for it

Verdict-engine accuracy recomputation: pending promotion. The screener page currently prints hit-rate figures from an older static paragraph; those figures are frozen copy from windows that have since rolled forward, not a live ledger, and this page does not restate them as current accuracy. A recomputed verdict-engine accuracy exists only as staged analysis: under the site's own rule, no engine change or accuracy figure is promoted until it clears re-verification, a portfolio simulation, at least 20 forward shadow resolutions, and review. Until then, the honest statement is that the recomputation is pending — not a number.

The same gate holds for the staged logic fixes (a confirmed-bearish pattern gate, a non-equity exclusion, a zero-volume guard, and a calls-ledger date fix). They are not live, they are not described here as improvements, and no figure derived from them appears on this page.

Ledger rule going forward: every published rate must reconcile exactly to per-call rows (engine, engine version, symbol, session, inputs, entry, stop, target, horizon, outcome, costs, corporate-action adjustments). Aggregate-only track records — the mechanism that let the frozen screener figures drift from the live ledger — are not used for any new accuracy claim.

6 · Data sources and limits

Market data comes from the site's own archive (daily OHLC, live tape, broker flows, corporate actions), cross-checked against independent sources on samples. The archive is the arbiter for chart history; where a source disagrees, the mismatch is reported, never force-fitted. Index and sub-index histories shown on this site are derived equal-weighted series unless labeled official. Delays: quotes are delayed snapshots during trading hours; figures shown are the last close when the market is shut.
