Quantitative Research Featured

NEPSE Technical Backtest Study

An independent quantitative study backtesting 19 rule-based trading systems on NEPSE index daily data from 2003 to 2026 (5,333 sessions). The headline finding: trend and momentum systems — RSI trend-ride (15.59% CAGR), EMA cross, Donchian breakout, and Bollinger breakout — beat buy & hold (12.87%) with materially smaller drawdowns, while mean-reversion systems lost money. I converted the findings into a rules-based trading plan: SMA200 regime filter, breakout entries, 1% risk per trade, and a two-loss weekly pause.
