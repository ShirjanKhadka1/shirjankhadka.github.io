How this board is built

RVOL ladder. For each rung N in 7 / 15 / 20 / 30 / 60 / 90 sessions, RVOL-N = today's volume ÷ the average volume of the prior N sessions (today excluded). RVOL-7 of 2.0 means the stock traded twice its last-week pace. Averages, shock ratios and the 20-session rolling high are computed from Nepse Decode's own OHLCV archive — the same archive behind the rest of this site — and the board is rebuilt after every market close.

PVCS (price–volume confirmation score) = percentage change × RVOL-7. It rewards moves that arrive on heavy volume and discounts moves on thin volume: a +5% day at RVOL 0.4 scores lower than a +2% day at RVOL 3.

VPD (volume pressure, 10 sessions) is Nepse Decode's own measure: 10 × (up-volume − down-volume) ÷ total volume over the last 10 sessions, where a session's volume counts as up or down by its close versus the previous close. It ranges from −10 (every session down on volume) to +10. Published volume-pressure formulas vary by provider and no canonical one exists, so we publish ours exactly as defined here rather than borrowing a black box.

Volume shock = today's volume ÷ yesterday's volume. Flags: Shock — shock ratio ≥ 3.0 or RVOL-20 ≥ 2.0. Expansion — 7-session average volume above the 30-session average with RVOL-7 ≥ 1.0. Compression — daily range ≤ 2.5% with RVOL-20 ≤ 0.7 (quiet, tight, waiting). Quiet accumulation — |daily change| ≤ 0.5% (price flat while the tape stays active). Signal tags read off RVOL-7: ≥ 3.5 high volume spike, ≥ 1.8 elevated, ≤ 0.5 volume dry-up, otherwise normal.

Coverage. Ordinary equities with at least 91 sessions of archived history that traded in the latest session. Mutual funds, debentures and promoter shares are excluded — their volume means something different. A small number of equities whose archived history is still being backfilled join the board automatically once their history is complete.

During market hours the board refreshes intraday from live quotes and is marked provisional: Volume is the cumulative shares traded so far today (shown next to yesterday's full-session volume), the RVOL denominators stay the prior-session averages, and PVCS uses the live price change. A stock joins the intraday board once it has traded. The final board — full-session volume, closing prices — replaces the provisional board after the close. Rolling High 20D is the highest traded price of the last 20 sessions including today.

Mechanical volume statistics for education, not investment advice. Unusual volume shows where attention went; it does not say whether the next move is up or down. Data: Nepse Decode OHLCV archive, rebuilt after each close.
