Does unusual volume predict anything?

GroupMean excessBeat rateSessions

How it is measured: every session, each stock is put in its groups (signal tag, top/bottom PVCS decile, VPD ≥ +5 / ≤ −5, quiet accumulation, shock). Forward return = close h sessions later on that stock's own traded bars, minus the equal-weight average board stock that session — so a group at 0.00pp merely matched the market. Windows containing a single-session move over 15% are dropped as corporate-action noise. Each session's group mean counts once in the aggregate (mean of session means), never pooled by headcount. A stock can sit in several groups at once; groups are lenses, not partitions. Nothing here changes the board's formula — tracking only measures it.

Methodology

How this board is built

RVOL ladder

For each rung N in 7 / 15 / 20 / 30 / 60 / 90 sessions, RVOL-N = today's volume ÷ the average volume of the prior N sessions (today excluded). RVOL-7 of 2.0 means the stock traded twice its last-week pace. The AVG column beside each rung is that prior-N-session average volume itself. Averages, shock ratios and the 20-session rolling high are computed from Nepse Decode's own OHLCV archive — the same archive behind the rest of this site — and the board is rebuilt after every market close.

PVCS

Price–volume confirmation score = percentage change × RVOL-7. It rewards moves that arrive on heavy volume and discounts moves on thin volume: a +5% day at RVOL 0.4 scores lower than a +2% day at RVOL 3.

VPD

Volume pressure, 10 sessions, is Nepse Decode's own measure: 10 × (up-volume − down-volume) ÷ total volume over the last 10 sessions, where a session's volume counts as up or down by its close versus the previous close. It ranges from −10 (every session down on volume) to +10. Published volume-pressure formulas vary by provider and no canonical one exists, so we publish ours exactly as defined here rather than borrowing a black box.

Volume shock & flags

Volume shock = today's volume ÷ yesterday's volume. Flags: Shock — shock ratio ≥ 3.0 or RVOL-20 ≥ 2.0. Expansion — 7-session average volume above the 30-session average with RVOL-7 ≥ 1.0. Compression — daily range ≤ 2.5% with RVOL-20 ≤ 0.7 (quiet, tight, waiting). Quiet accumulation — |daily change| ≤ 0.5% (price flat while the tape stays active). Flags are independent: a stock can carry several at once. Signal tags read off RVOL-7: ≥ 3.5 high volume spike, ≥ 1.8 elevated, ≤ 0.5 volume dry-up, otherwise normal.

Coverage

Ordinary equities with at least 91 sessions of archived history that traded in the latest session. Mutual funds, debentures and promoter shares are excluded — their volume means something different. A small number of equities whose archived history is still being backfilled join the board automatically once their history is complete.

During market hours

The board refreshes intraday from live quotes and is marked provisional: Volume is the cumulative shares traded so far today (shown next to yesterday's full-session volume), the RVOL denominators stay the prior-session averages, and PVCS uses the live price change. A stock joins the intraday board once it has traded. The final board — full-session volume, closing prices — replaces the provisional board after the close. Rolling High 20D is the highest traded price of the last 20 sessions including today.
