Method

How this page is built

Source (one page, published files only): the Capital Max MF Screener (login-gated portal) is read through a saved login at snapshot time and served from this site's own data files — visitors need no login, and the page never calls the source live. Each snapshot carries the NAV Statement view and the Sector Allocation view exactly as captured.

Snapshot-only, history from now on: the source publishes no history and no date picker, so there is no earlier history to show. Snapshots accumulate daily from 29 Oct 2026 onward; earlier dates render honestly unavailable, never backfilled or estimated.

Premium / discount definition: (Market Price − Weekly NAV) ÷ Weekly NAV, as published by the source. Recomputing it from the two-decimal figures shown here can differ by about 0.01 percentage points, because the source computes from unrounded values (C30MF: published −0.50%, recomputed −0.51%). The page shows the source's published figure, never a recomputed replacement.

Open-end funds [OEF]: per the source's footnote, open-end funds have no market price and no sector allocation — every such cell is “–”. Funds without the [OEF] tag but with no market price in this snapshot (GRSY, MSIP, SEOS, SIGS1) are shown the same way, not guessed at.

Empty rows are kept: SIGS1 is an entirely empty row in both source views and stays in the table as a row of “–” values, never dropped silently. LVF1 has a market price but no weekly NAV, so its premium/discount is “–”. SEOS has a monthly NAV only.

Average sector mix: the 29 Oct 2026 Average sector row sums to 127.07%, not 100% — it is an aggregate across funds, not a single fund's allocation. It is shown exactly as published, never recomputed or “fixed”. Per-fund sector rows do sum to ~100% and are validated on every snapshot.

Not in this snapshot, with reason: fund size, maturity date/period and fund manager appear on the source page but were not transcribed in the 29 Oct 2026 capture; sector market values (Rs) were likewise not transcribed — only percentages. The snapshot schema already carries these fields, so a future capture can add them without a page change. Holdings detail and holding changes are unavailable at the source itself (those sections are broken on the source site) and are never shown here.

Refresh: the snapshot list re-checks every 60 seconds; a newly published snapshot appears as a note, and the current view is never yanked away.
