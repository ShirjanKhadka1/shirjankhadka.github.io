Bars compare commission and SEBON rates against the top 0.36% slab only — the DP charge is a flat fee and CGT applies to gains, not trade value, so neither sits on this scale. CGT was halved by a Ministry of Finance notice in the Nepal Gazette (vol. 76, Additional 40, Part 5, dated 31 Bhadra 2083 — 16 September 2026), replacing the Finance Act 2083 rates of 10% / 7.5%. The gain is taxed after fees: sell proceeds after charges, minus the fee-inclusive cost of buying. Rates above are for resident individuals on listed shares; dividends are taxed separately at a flat 5%. If any rate changes, this table and the calculators change together — the date of the schedule is part of the page.

Price adjustment — bonus & right shares

Bonus adjustment: price ÷ (1 + bonus%). Right adjustment: (price + face value × right%) ÷ (1 + right%). These are the standard NEPSE reference-price formulas applied after book closure.
