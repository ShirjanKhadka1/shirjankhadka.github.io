How the signals work

▲ Uptrend signal

The 20-day average crosses above the 50-day average. In a 23-year backtest of NEPSE, trend-following rules like this beat buy-and-hold with smaller drawdowns.

▼ Downtrend signal

The 20-day average crosses below the 50-day average — momentum has rolled over and capital preservation takes priority.

RSI (14)

Above 70 suggests overbought conditions; below 30 suggests oversold. Best read together with the trend, not alone.

An honest note: this is not a live tick chart. NEPSE publishes no free public real-time feed, so the 1-second/5-second charts you see on broker terminals (like nepsealpha) can't be reproduced here — those run on licensed data. This lab instead gives you the complete daily history with professional-grade indicators, computed transparently in your browser.

Signals are mechanical outputs of a backtested rule set, shown for education. They are not investment advice, not AI predictions, and past performance does not guarantee future results.

Questions

Is this a live NEPSE chart?

It uses daily data through 18 September 2026 — the full published history — rather than live ticks. Broker terminals show second-level data because they license a real-time feed; no free public equivalent exists.

What timeframes are available?

1M, 3M, 6M, 1Y on daily candles; 5Y and All switch to weekly candles so two decades stay readable.

Are the signals AI-generated?

No. They are simple, fully transparent rules (SMA 20/50 crossovers) drawn from a published 23-year backtest of NEPSE systems. You can verify every marker against the moving averages on the chart.

Where does the data come from?

Daily OHLCV of the NEPSE index, 17 July 2003 – 18 September 2026 (5,333 sessions), from the open nepse-open-data archive used in my technical study.
