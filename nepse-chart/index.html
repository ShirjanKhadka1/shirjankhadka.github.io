How the signal engine works

Ten transparent factors

Trend vs SMA 50/200, recent golden/death crosses, RSI zone, MACD momentum, turnover participation, 52-week position, divergences, chart patterns, the NEPSE index backdrop, and support/resistance proximity. Each factor's points are shown — no black box.

Buy / Hold / Exit

Factor scores add up to a verdict from Strong Buy to Strong Exit. It is a mechanical summary of what the daily chart looks like right now — a starting point for research, not a recommendation.

Live when the market is open

During NEPSE hours (Sun–Thu, 9:30–15:00 NPT) the quote panel refreshes from a free community feed and the chart includes the session in progress. Otherwise you see the last closed session.

An honest note: NEPSE publishes no free official real-time API, so tick-level data like broker terminals show is not possible here — quotes come from free community mirrors and can be delayed. What this lab does instead: full daily history for any listed stock, professional indicators, pattern/divergence detection and a fully transparent scoring model, all computed in your browser.

All signals are mechanical outputs of published rules, shown for education. They are not investment advice, not AI predictions, and past performance does not guarantee future results.

Questions

Does this give buy or sell signals?

It shows a rule-based verdict — Strong Buy, Buy, Hold, Exit/Reduce or Strong Exit — with every contributing factor listed. Transparent and educational; not financial advice.

Which patterns are detected?

Double tops and bottoms, head & shoulders (and inverse), ascending/descending/symmetrical triangles, rising/falling wedges, plus regular and hidden bullish/bearish divergences on RSI(14) and the MACD histogram.

Is the data real-time?

Quotes refresh during market hours from a free community feed (LIVE badge); outside hours you see the last closed session. History is daily OHLC per stock.

What timeframes are available?

1M, 3M, 6M, 1Y, 2Y on daily candles; 5Y and All switch to weekly candles so long histories stay readable.

Where does the data come from?

Per-stock daily OHLC from the open Nepse-All-Scraper dataset, live quotes and monthly LTP history from the yonepse open API, and the NEPSE index daily history (2003–2026, 5,333 sessions) from the nepse-open-data archive. Coverage spans every currently listed security — equities, mutual funds, debentures and preference shares. Securities without scraper OHLC (e.g. newer listings) use LTP-only history and are clearly flagged, with pattern/divergence detection disabled for those. With thanks to both open-data projects.
