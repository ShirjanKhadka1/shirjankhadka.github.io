Method

How this page is built

Sources (all published, no new feed): live quotes (live.json), verdict batch (verdicts.json), listed universe (universe.json), per-symbol closes (history files), listed totals and splits (shareholding.json), verified bonus/right events (adjustments.json), index archive (index-history.json).

Returns rule: only closes on/after 15 Jul 2026 are used — earlier bars exist but are not independently verified, so a period whose reference bar would fall before that date shows “—”, never an estimate. Closes before a verified bonus/right ex-date are adjusted by the event factor, exactly as the stock-page ladders do; cash dividends are never reinvested, so these are bonus/right-adjusted price returns, not total returns.

Day change is the live quote's day change. The verdict batch's change figure (a different, raw basis on bonus ex-dates) is never shown beside it.

Not compared here, with reason: market capitalisation — the quote feed publishes it as null, so showing it would fabricate; fundamentals (PE, EPS, book value) — they live on each stock page's fundamentals section and update quarterly, not with market data.

Refresh: quotes and verdicts re-check every 60 seconds and repaint only when their fingerprint changes; history files are daily and update after close.
