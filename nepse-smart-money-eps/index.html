Nothing here is a call: skilled money beside an earnings trend is a description, not a prediction.

How this board is built

A descriptive reading of skilled broker flow beside earnings, for education, not investment advice. Data: Nepse Decode's own floorsheet archive and published quarterly filings, rebuilt after each close. No live feed is claimed: during market hours this page still shows the last close's board.
