Frequently Asked Questions

Is this kundali calculator free?

Yes, completely free. No signup, no paywall, no locked features. Everything is available to everyone.

How accurate are the calculations?

We use the Lahiri ayanamsha with VSOP87 planetary theory and ELP/MPP02 lunar theory, validated against the Swiss Ephemeris to within 0.05 degrees. The mean lunar node is used and disclosed.

Is my birth data private?

Yes. All calculations happen in your browser using JavaScript. Your birth details are never transmitted to any server.

What is kundali matching?

Our marriage matching section compares two birth charts using the traditional 36-point Ashtakoota (Guna Milan) system, showing every koota score with the rule behind it, plus a Mangal Dosha check for both people. Remember, Guna Milan is one Moon-based factor among many, not a final judgment on marriage.
