What “holdings” means here

A broker's net position in a stock is measured transaction flow over the selected period: everything that broker's clients bought, minus everything they sold, from the period's broker net-flow records. A positive net means the broker bought more than it sold (accumulating); a negative net means it sold more (distributing); zero means flat.

This is not verified shareholding and not beneficial ownership. Clients trade through a broker, so a net position does not mean the broker owns those shares. Nothing here is a forecast or a call — it is a measurement of what already traded, updated as new sessions publish.

Broker history starts 2026-07-15, so 3M and longer views all cover the same sessions (2026-07-15 → the latest session) — the page says so rather than pretending a longer window exists. Traded totals are corrected totals: the period files double-count per-symbol value and quantity, so this page shows value ÷ 2 and quantity ÷ 2 (proven against official turnover).
