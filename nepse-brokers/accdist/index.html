What “accumulating” and “distributing” mean here

Each row is one broker–stock pair over the selected period. A pair is accumulating when that broker's clients bought more than they sold (positive net transaction value) and distributing when they sold more than they bought (negative net value). Net value is measured transaction flow from the period's broker net-flow records — bought minus sold, nothing estimated.

Each list shows the top 200 pairs by net value: accumulating ranked largest net buy first, distributing ranked largest net sell first. Pairs below the cut-off are not shown — on 1D, 4,854 accumulating pairs and 5,641 distributing pairs exist in the full flow, so the lists are the top 200 of each, not the whole market. Zero-net pairs appear in neither list.

This is not verified shareholding and not beneficial ownership, and nothing here is a forecast or a call — it is a measurement of what already traded. Broker history starts 2026-07-15, so 3M and longer views all cover the same sessions (2026-07-15 to the latest session) rather than pretending a longer window. Net values are single-counted nets (buy minus sell); they are never halved and never doubled.
