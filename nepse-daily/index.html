How to read this table. Every row is one listed security as of the last closed session. Click a column header to sort — click again to reverse. Top gainers sorts by % change, highest first; top losers lowest first. Day range shows the session low and high. Turnover is in rupees.

Open is shown as Unavailable because the market feed does not publish opening prices — we never estimate or fill in a number. Figures may be delayed; the session date above is the data's own date, not the time you loaded the page.

Two honest gaps, not errors. Previous close is the tape's own previous_close figure; where the validated end-of-day close field differs by a paisa-class rounding (0.01), both figures stay as their sources report them and neither is forced to match. Turnover here is the tape's own sum; the broker floorsheet total can differ by a fraction of a percent through aggregation, not dropped symbols. Market capitalisation is NEPSE's official published session total from its market summary.
