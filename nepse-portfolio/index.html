    About the NEPSE portfolio tracker

    The NEPSE portfolio tracker keeps your holdings honestly counted. Add any of the 410 listed securities with the quantity you bought and the price you paid; adding more of a security you already hold updates its weighted average cost. The holdings table shows your average cost, the latest batch price, market value and unrealized P&L per line, so every rupee is accounted for.

    Recorded sales land in their own ledger with realized P&L computed against the average cost at the time of sale. Allocation views break your portfolio across securities and sectors so concentration is visible at a glance, the analytics panel summarizes winners, losers and overall return, and a printable report button produces a clean one-page summary for your records.

    Your data never leaves your device: holdings are saved in your browser’s local storage, not on any server, and there is no account to create. Prices come from the latest daily batch — during market hours they can lag the live market, and a holding with no price in the latest batch shows as – and is left out of the totals rather than guessed.

    This is bookkeeping, not advice. The tracker does the arithmetic; it never tells you what to buy or sell. All P&L figures are mechanical outputs for education — verify everything against your broker’s statements before acting on it.

    Portfolio tracker questions

    Where is my portfolio data stored?

    Only in your own browser, using local storage. Nothing is uploaded to any server and no account is needed — but clearing your browser data will erase it, so the printable report is there for your records.

    How is average cost calculated?

    Weighted average cost: the total amount paid for a security divided by the total quantity held. Each new buy updates it; each recorded sale computes realized P&L against the average cost at the time of sale.

    Are portfolio prices real-time?

    No. Prices come from the latest daily batch, which can lag during trading hours. NEPSE publishes no free official real-time API, so community mirrors and batch data may be delayed.

    Is this investment advice?

    No. The tracker is arithmetic for education — it never recommends buying or selling. Verify figures with your broker before any decision.
