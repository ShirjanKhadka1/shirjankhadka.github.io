Nothing is backfilled: tracking started 26 Oct 2026, so the first resolutions arrive when October/Kartik close in the data. A prediction with no sign (average exactly 0) can never match or miss.

Sector method: each composite chains equal-weighted daily returns of the sector's currently listed constituent stocks from our own OHLC archive (base 1000). A day counts only when at least 3 constituents traded; daily moves beyond the ±10% circuit band and returns spanning a suspension gap of more than 20 days are excluded as reference-price resets, not market moves. Monthly return = month-end level vs the previous available month-end level — across the 2020 COVID closure, that return spans the closure. NEPSE's own monthly series is capped at the archive's latest session, so a stray index-history row past the archive cannot create a phantom month. These composites are reconstructions, not the official NEPSE sub-indices, and membership is today's applied historically, so early years are thinner — the contributing-stock count is printed per sector so the coverage is never hidden. History is a tendency, not a forecast. For education only — not investment advice.

Method: month-end to month-end log-free simple returns on the NEPSE index close. Averages are arithmetic means across calendar years with a complete month. Coverage and year counts are shown per row — months with fewer years carry less weight. Seasonality is a historical tendency, not a forecast: regimes, policy and liquidity change the pattern.
